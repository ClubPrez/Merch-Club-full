How we run construction apparel programs
Most construction teams don’t need another vendor.
They need someone to own the process.
1. Program planning
We define apparel tiers, decoration methods, and rollout strategy aligned with your company structure.
2. Controlled design and proofing
Logos are placed correctly. Decoration methods are selected intentionally. Consistency matters across garments and colors.
3. Production oversight
We select products based on durability, use case, and environment. Embroidery, screen print, patch, or specialty decoration — chosen based on how it will actually perform.
4. Distribution and reordering systems
Bulk delivery to job sites. Department distribution. Or structured reorder systems for ongoing needs.
