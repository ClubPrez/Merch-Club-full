How we run multi-location merchandise programs
Scaling branded merchandise requires process.
1. Brand standard alignment
We align with marketing leadership to define logo usage, garment standards, decoration methods, and program scope.
2. Controlled proofing and production
Every item follows defined brand guidelines. Production is monitored for consistency across batches and facilities.
3. Distribution coordination
Bulk shipments, location-based kits, or structured reorder systems depending on organizational needs.
4. Ongoing program oversight
We help maintain consistency long term, not just at launch.
