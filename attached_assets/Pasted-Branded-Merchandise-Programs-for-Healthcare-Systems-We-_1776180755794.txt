How we run healthcare merchandise programs
You shouldn’t have to manage five vendors to execute one initiative.
Here’s how we structure it.
1. Strategic alignment
We align with marketing and operations leadership to define scope, timelines, brand standards, and distribution needs.
2. Controlled design and proofing
Brand accuracy matters in healthcare. We manage the proofing process to maintain consistency across apparel, print, and packaging.
3. Production oversight
We source products intentionally. Decoration methods are selected based on durability, environment, and usage. Quality is monitored before anything ships.
4. Fulfillment and distribution
Kitting. Multi-location shipping. Department-level coordination. We manage logistics so internal teams aren’t chasing boxes.
Execution is structured from start to finish.
